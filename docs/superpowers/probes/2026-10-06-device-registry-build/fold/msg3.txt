fix(devices): setup records how zone and distributor devices name the hub

The hub's registration already returns its device entry; setup now keeps its id
and records hub_link_for's answer in hass.data before the platforms are set up,
afresh on every setup, since hass.data outlives a reload. On Home Assistant
2026.8 and later the zone and distributor devices therefore name the hub by
registry id, and the via_device deprecation goes away; below 2026.8 the
identifier form stays as before.
