Pre-release on upstream master 7001c754 (v2026.10.04 plus the merged weather-sensor liveness check), with the seasonal-outlook fix of v2026.10.05b2. New: zone and distributor devices name their hub by registry id, and deleting a zone or a distributor finds its device per config entry, wherever Home Assistant offers both calls (2026.8 and later). This removes the `via_device` deprecation warning that Home Assistant 2026.9 logs at startup and the `async_get_device` warning on a delete; both old calls stop working in 2027.8. Below 2026.8 nothing changes. Existing devices keep the same hub as their parent. No storage change compared with v2026.10.05b2.
